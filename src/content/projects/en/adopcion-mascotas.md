## The problem

Many shelters and individuals post pets for adoption in scattered places, with no central hub where someone interested can easily search, filter and get in touch. The goal was to build an end-to-end MVP of the adoption process, prioritizing the essentials to reach a tangible, usable result quickly.

## What we built

We designed the full prototype in Figma and then brought it to code: a site where anyone can post a pet for adoption, search by species, province, size, status and sex, and move forward with the adoption process from each animal's profile.

- Posting and editing adoptable pet profiles
- Search and filters by species, province, size, status and sex
- Profiles with each pet's bio, age, weight and temperament
- "Adopt" flow and saving favorites

## My role

My main contribution was on the back office: I collaborated on the site's backend development. I also contributed to the front office, adding UX/UI design details to the Figma prototype and making some changes directly in the frontend.
